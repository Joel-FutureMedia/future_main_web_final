About Us Page:

People First. 
Media That Matters.

Futuremedia is a Namibian media company with a simple purpose: connecting people with people. From radio and television to print, innovative digital solutions and live events, our teams share stories that matter to local communities and help you connect your brand with audiences across Namibia.

Where it began 

A Rare Inheritance 
When Futuremedia was established in 2019, it brought together brands that already held a place in Namibians’ lives. Omulunga Radio, Radiowave, 99FM, Fresh FM, Nova 103.5, OneAfrica and Property News were familiar names. The real inheritance was the trust Namibians had placed in them over the years.
Together, these brands reached more than two million Namibians, over 70% of the country’s population, across every major language group, demographic and region. That reach came with a responsibility to the people behind those numbers. How could we build on their trust and use our network to bring Namibians closer together?
